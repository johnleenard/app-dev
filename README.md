Tanjiro embarks on a dangerous journey to find a cure for Nezuko and avenge his family. Along the way, he trains under the Demon Slayer Corps, facing off against powerful demons while uncovering dark secrets about the demonic world. The show is praised for its breathtaking animation, especially in its action sequences, and its emotional storytelling. It has become one of the most popular anime series globally, with its blend of action, fantasy, and heartfelt moments.
